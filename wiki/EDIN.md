# Journey Map: activities the EDIN users do

## Key Activities

Fighters can input their urgent needs into a mailbox => inspiration for this whole story !!! => need standardized form
Collect feedback from military end-users and merge with national projects

MoDs use "scouting tools" to discover new tech and how to (counter)act on this info

Benchmark of the EU Ecosystem

Require funding/finances, New EU Finance Instrument

## Tasks/Steps for the activities

1. Fighters fill in form/template and sends the mail to EDIN user (discovery) => Problem statement / Formalized Requests (20/month roughly)
2. EDIN users evaluates and reads email
3. Round of internal and external consultation (can be with endusers themselves) ensues. Depending on frequency of type of problem, they check around who would also be helped with this type of solution/problem. Identify scale of problems. 
=> Pain point how does this happen??? 
=> Here the matchmaking platform will have its real value
4. EDIN user(s) identify if there are potential solutions out there (always first on national level) 
=> Another pain point, no actual solution at this moment???
5. Money is always an issue, Try to solve it within their capabilities and only then they look on the outside.
6. Committee decide to solve issue national or international => give their recommendation
7. If national (public procurement, no EDA involvement), if recommendation international then HEDI (EDA) will be approached
8. (continuing with international solution) They will contact HEDI with email exchange, no standardization
9. Essentially partnerships (!!!) within EDA/MS need to be found for the solution. Within EDA, an expert group is gathered depending on the specifics of the issue.
=> what happens after it becomes fuzzy, no real actual solution after this step. Typically if there is no clear follow-up, look for national solution
=> With regards to R&D there is more willingness to cooperate
=> More willingness to cooperate with SMEs
